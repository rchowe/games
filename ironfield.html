How to Play

Build a maze

Enemies march from the entry gates (arrows pointing in) to the exits. Towers can go on any open grass tile, and enemies walk around them — so place towers to force long, winding routes. You can never block the path completely. The faint dashed line shows the current route.

Towers

Gatling — fast all-rounder. Flamethrower — cone of fire, ignores armor. Cryo — slows. Tesla Coil — chain lightning. Mortar — long-range splash. Missile Pod — homing, great vs aircraft. Railgun — pierces everything in a line.

Flying units ignore your maze and fly straight to the exit. Flamethrowers, Cryo and Mortars can't hit them.

Controls

Click a tower button (or press 1–7), then click the field to build. Click a tower to upgrade (U) or sell (S). Right-click or Esc cancels. Space calls the next wave early for bonus gold, F changes speed.

Versus

One player hosts and shares the 5-digit code; the other joins. Both fields face the same escalating waves. Spend gold on the send panel (Z X C V B N M) to unleash monsters on your opponent — each send raises the income you collect every 6 seconds. Bigger monsters unlock as the match goes on.
