How to Play

WASD — Move
Mouse — Aim
Left Click — Basic attack (hold)
Right Click — Class skill
Q / E — Skills
R — Ultimate
Space — Dodge roll (invulnerable)
1 — Drink Ember Flask
F — Interact / Revive ally
Tab — Hero sheet, gear & skills
Esc — Menu

When a foe winds up a major attack, a glowing red template appears on the ground. When it fills, it strikes. Roll out — or roll through it with perfect timing. Your own great attacks show blue templates, and clever foes will try to escape them.

Fallen allies can be revived by standing over them and holding F. If the whole party falls, you return to the last Ember Shrine.
